Platinum Roadmap

Grand Theft Auto VI — 55 trophies — Leonida / Vice City

← Back to guide

This page is a plan, not a checklist of secrets. Follow the phases in order and the platinum will come to you instead of the other way around.

Phase 1 — Story First

Play the campaign as Lucia and Jason from start to finish. Learn the map, unlock the open world, and enjoy the story without hunting anything.

Time: roughly 30–40 hours. Difficulty: easy — just play.

Phase 2 — Side Content

Clear side missions, activities and stranger encounters as you find them. Anything tied to a story chapter should be done before that chapter ends.

Time: roughly 15–20 hours. Difficulty: moderate.

Phase 3 — Collectibles

Work through the collectible sets region by region so you never double back across the whole state. Finish one district before moving on.

Time: roughly 10–15 hours. Difficulty: moderate — patience, not skill.

Phase 4 — Cleanup

Mop up whatever is left: grinding goals, challenge completions and any trophy that needs a specific setup. Use this phase to replay short missions.

Time: roughly 5–10 hours. Difficulty: varies — hardest trophies live here.

The Missable Rule

Some content can only be done at a certain point in the story. The rule is simple: when the game offers you a one-time activity, do it now. Never assume you can come back later, and keep a manual save before big story choices.
