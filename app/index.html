    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough is enough: there are no online trophies and no difficulty trophies. The order below keeps the missable trophy safe and leaves the long grinds for after the credits, when everything is open. Numbers marked “reported” come from launch-week guides, not from Rockstar.

    Save rules that protect the run

    • Keep one manual save at the start of every chapter, plus one untouched save from just before the final mission. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus.
    • Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — it counts toward Positive Mental Attitude and disappears after that chapter.
    • Do Race Against Yago the moment it appears in Chapter 3 (hourglass icon) — it is time-sensitive.
    • Never use cheats on your trophy save: cheats are reported to switch off trophies and saving.
    • The 100% checklist appears after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but work you do earlier still counts.

    Sources & how the list was verified

    Grand Theft Auto VI is developed and published by Rockstar Games. This is an independent fan-made guide, not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used.

    Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have not published a trophy list on their own channels. Every trophy name, official description and grade in this guide was verified on 19 November 2026 against the live PlayStation trophy list as mirrored by Exophase (55 entries, with real player unlock rarity) and cross-checked against five independent full lists that agree on all 55 names, descriptions and grades: OutputLag, HowToVideoGame, Press-Start, GamesRadar+ and PC Gamer, and GTABase. Push Square also reported the 55-trophy count from Exophase on 18 November 2026.

    Five hidden trophies have no confirmed unlock method yet at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their guides say so honestly instead of inventing a method. Figures marked “reported” in the roadmap (for example the 35-task 100% checklist, 18 meditation spots, 42 Shooting Range gold medals) come from launch-week guides, not from official sources. Xbox Gamerscore values are not shown: published values conflict between sources.
